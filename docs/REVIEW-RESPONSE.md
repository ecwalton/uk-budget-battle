# Response to the independent review

Date: 5 September 2026

The review supports proceeding with conditions. Version 2 of the concept brief incorporates its strongest recommendations while changing several proposed fixes that would introduce unsupported assumptions. This is a design revision, not a claim that a model has been built or tested.

## Changes to the proposed tests

- Replace “capital cuts must not rank in the top third” with tests that deferred costs and plausible long-term effects are included. A prescribed ranking is a policy preference, not an accounting test.
- Replace “efficiency yields must be less than half the gap” with checks against supported card costs, limits and delivery assumptions.
- Compare baseline and player under the same shock. Do not demand an identical choice score across shocks: policy and economic conditions can interact.
- Replace equal political costs for equal-sized policies with transparent assumptions and presentation-invariance tests. Version 1 avoids this scoring problem by excluding political support from success.
- Do not apply a universal behavioural haircut or lag to all taxes. Official estimates may already include behaviour.
- Show the future funding gap when recurring spending uses a one-off receipt. Such a choice need not be prohibited.
- Use a small learning study as formative evidence. A repeated pre/post quiz alone does not establish that the game caused lasting learning.

## Source checks and limits

The review's statement that John Healey became Chancellor on 20 July 2026 is supported by his [official GOV.UK profile](https://www.gov.uk/government/people/john-healey). Named ministers are unnecessary for the core simulation, so version 2 keeps a generic Chancellor role.

The [Treasury block-grant explanation](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/block-grant-transparency-october-2025/block-grant-transparency-october-2025-explanatory-note) supports programme-level comparability and population-based calculations, rather than a blanket percentage on every English spending decision.

The [OBR balance-sheet discussion](https://obr.uk/frs/fiscal-risks-and-sustainability-july-2025/) supports treating financial asset values and liabilities together. A sale at equivalent recognised value generally exchanges assets; it is not an automatic reduction in PSNFL.

This follow-up did not independently verify every new number in the submitted review. Its additional gross-interest amount, pension territorial qualification, SEND figure, exact Barnett estimate, tax timing, rate sensitivity and French gameplay details must not be treated as approved data. Numerical calibrations and causal service effects remain open work before implementation.

## Next review request

Review version 2 for unresolved accounting errors and whether its proposed acceptance criteria can be implemented with available evidence. Prioritise missing data that blocks specific cards. Do not reward the revision merely for acknowledging uncertainty: identify which unknowns prevent a credible prototype or public release.

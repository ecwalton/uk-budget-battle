# Preset specification and model results

The envelope game is at `/`; the separate GBTT programme walkthrough is at `/programme.html`. `/explorer.html` remains compatible. Presets are editable five-year drafts, not auto-confirmed results. Each year sets all nine levers; hold adds no new change and earlier commitments continue. Reset this Budget clears its draft only. Resume keeps the preset for future Budgets. Selecting a preset never changes the shock or engine thresholds.

## Mapping

### GBTT plan

Smaller spending envelopes, then tax relief. £52bn recurring spending cuts and £30bn tax cuts by Budget 3: the closest discrete translation of the £50bn/£30bn targets. Welfare −£16bn, defence −£12bn and other services −£24bn. These are authored allocations, not an approved GBTT costing. Deregulation, net-zero policy and migration are not fiscal levers in this engine; no savings from them are assumed.

| Budget | Changes (all others hold) |
|---|---|
| 1 | welfare:-1, defence:-1, other:-1 |
| 2 | other:-1 |
| 3 | income:-1, vat:-1 |
| 4 | All hold |
| 5 | All hold |

### Revenue first

Protect cash spending envelopes. Raise the income-tax package in each of the first three Budgets: £45bn annual revenue by Budget 3. No inflation protection or service improvement is assumed. The household effects of higher taxes still count.

| Budget | Changes (all others hold) |
|---|---|
| 1 | income:1 |
| 2 | income:1 |
| 3 | income:1 |
| 4 | All hold |
| 5 | All hold |

### Invest and tax

Grow investment once, raise income tax in all five Budgets and the business/wealth package once. Trade higher taxes for capacity. Investment maintenance arrives later; business receipts may disappoint. No automatic growth dividend funds the plan.

The GBTT translation funds every Budget and passes the term, capacity and household checks, but fails the £40bn year-five target and £200bn legacy target. Its £52bn/£30bn combination leaves only £22bn annual improvement. Achieving the £40bn target with £30bn tax relief would require at least £70bn recurring savings before any additional costs, under this engine. This is accounting, not evidence that such savings are deliverable.

Timing/comprehension study remains outstanding. Shocks retain the existing selectable scenarios; no random shock or parliamentary confidence mechanic has been added.
